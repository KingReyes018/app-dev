# My Favorite Series: Ted Lasso

## Overview
**Ted Lasso** (2020-2023) is a *feel-good comedy-drama* about an American college football coach who is hired to manage **AFC Richmond**, an English soccer club, despite knowing almost nothing about the sport.

---

## Why I Like It
- Kind, optimistic characters without being cheesy
- Funny and emotional at the same time
- Teamwork and mental health are treated seriously
- Symbolism of the characters favorite words that has meaningful meanings that is use in everyday life

## Main Characters
1. Ted Lasso 
2. Coach Beard 
3. Rebecca Welton 
4. Roy Kent 
5. Keeley Jones
6. Jamie Tartt 
7. Nate Shelley
